Data Deletion Request

We respect your privacy and your right to control your personal data. If you would like to delete your AnaLog Your Ride account and all associated data, you can submit a request using the information below.

How to Request Data Deletion

Send an email to our support team with the subject line "Data Deletion Request":

support@analogyourride.com

Please include the email address associated with your AnaLog Your Ride account so we can locate and delete your data.

What Data Will Be Deleted

When you request account deletion, we will permanently delete:

  • Your account information (email, username, profile photo)
  • All trips and routes you have created
  • Your gear lists
  • Ride history and tracking data
  • Photos you have uploaded
  • Trail reports you have submitted
  • Gear Swap listings
  • Trail Angel profile (if applicable)
  • All other content associated with your account
Please Note: Data deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Once your data is deleted, it cannot be recovered. Please make sure to export any data you wish to keep before submitting a deletion request.

Processing Time

We will process your data deletion request within 30 days of receiving it. You will receive a confirmation email once your data has been deleted.

Data Retained for Legal Purposes

In some cases, we may be required to retain certain data for legal, regulatory, or security purposes. This may include:

  • Transaction records (if applicable)
  • Data required for ongoing legal proceedings
  • Data necessary to protect against fraud or abuse

Any retained data will be kept secure and deleted once no longer required.

Questions?

If you have any questions about data deletion or our privacy practices, please contact us at support@analogyourride.com or review our Privacy Policy.

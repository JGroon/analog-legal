This Privacy Policy describes how AnaLog Your Ride ("AnaLog," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you use our mobile application and related services (the "Service").

By using AnaLog Your Ride, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide

Account Information:

  • Email address
  • Username and display name
  • Profile photo (optional)
  • Password (encrypted)

Trip and Route Data:

  • Routes you create, including waypoints and pins
  • Trip details (name, dates, notes)
  • Gear lists
  • Photos you upload to trips

Ride Tracking Data:

  • GPS coordinates during tracked rides
  • Ride duration, distance, and elevation
  • Photos and journal entries

Community Content:

  • Trail reports and condition updates
  • Trail Angel profiles
  • Gear Swap listings
  • Comments and messages

1.2 Information Collected Automatically

Location Data:

  • GPS location when using maps or tracking rides
  • Location is only collected when the app is in use and you've granted permission
  • You can disable location access in your device settings

Device Information:

  • Device type and operating system
  • App version
  • Crash logs and performance data

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide the Service: Display maps, save your trips, track rides, and enable community features
  • Improve the Service: Analyze usage patterns, fix bugs, and develop new features
  • Communicate: Send service updates, respond to support requests
  • Ensure Safety: Detect and prevent fraud, abuse, or violations of our Terms
  • Personalize: Remember your preferences and settings
We do not:
  • Sell your personal information to third parties
  • Use your data for targeted advertising
  • Share your precise location with other users without your consent
